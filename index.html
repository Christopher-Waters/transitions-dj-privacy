Privacy Policy

Transitions DJ · Effective 22 November 2026

Transitions DJ is a DJ and automatic mixing app. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

Information we collect

None. Transitions DJ does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Your music

When you add songs, from your Files, by drag and drop, or from your device’s music library, the app keeps a copy inside its own private storage on your device so the songs are still there the next time you open it. Your songs, playlists, settings and the tempo/key analysis of your tracks never leave your device. Removing a song from the app, or deleting the app, removes these copies.

Permissions

Music library (iPhone/iPad): used only to import the songs you choose. The app does not read anything else in your library.

Background audio: used so your mix keeps playing when the screen is locked.

Network

The app does not send any data over the internet.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, the app collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
